# Overarching idea

The game is a tamagotchi-like game, but focused on Tia

# How it's unique to Tia

- You feed the Tia Pizza
- The player is a tia
- The tia has kitty friends
- Has an eepiness factor
- Takes lots of naps
- Can play the sims

# Game Loop:

## Egg Stage

1. The game starts with an egg, which you need to press the main button to hatch.
2. It shakes for a minute or so, then out comes a baby Tia.

# Baby Stage

1. The baby Tia is very small and needs a lot of care. You need to feed it pizza, play with it, and make sure it gets enough eep.
2. The baby Tia will grow over time
3. The player will get alerts as the baby Tia grows, such as "Your Tia is hungry!" or "Your Tia needs to nap!"

# Adult Stage

1. Once the Tia reaches adulthood, it will have more complex needs and behaviors. It will still need to be fed pizza and get enough eep, but it will also have social needs, such as playing with its kitty friends and interacting with other Tias.

# Keep Alive

1. The player needs to keep the Tia alive by feeding it pizza, making sure it gets enough eep, and keeping it happy. If the Tia's needs are not met, it will become unhappy and eventually die.

# Bars

- Hunger Bar: Indicates how hungry the Tia is. It decreases over time and needs to be replenished by feeding it pizza.
- Eep Bar: Indicates how much eep the Tia has. It decreases over time and needs to be replenished by letting the Tia nap.
- Happiness Bar: Indicates how happy the Tia is. It decreases if the Tia's needs are not met and increases when the Tia is fed, gets enough eep, and interacts with its kitty friends.

# Characters/Sprites

- Tia
  - Baby Tia
  - Adult Tia (still little)
- Kitty Friends
- Egg
- Pizza
- Eep Bar
- Hunger Bar
- Happiness Bar

# Ways to make Tia happy

- Feed her pizza
- Let her nap
- Play with her kitty friends
- Pat the Tia
- Play a game with her (like the sims) or the dinosaur game
